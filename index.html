About the work

Beyond the limits
of conditioned
thought.

Enslaved Minds: Conscious & Unconscious is a pamphlet by Stewart Partlow-El that considers thought beyond the conditioned processes people use and the familiar concept of time.

Drawing on his understanding of Moorish Science and the teachings of Noble Drew Ali, Partlow-El explores national identity, spiritual understanding, and the relationship between the conscious and unconscious mind.

His stated purpose is to address confusion surrounding the spirit and its spiritual essence, inviting readers to examine these ideas through study and reflection.

The author’s journey

A life of study.
A search for
understanding.

Stewart Partlow-El traces his introduction to Moorish Science to 1989, when he became a Moorish American at the Occoquan facility in Lorton, Virginia, under Grand Sheik Earl Walker-El.

He continued his journey at the Maryland Correctional Institution in Hagerstown, learning from Moorish scholars from Baltimore, including Daryl Johnson-Bey and Michael Evans-El.

In 2005, while incarcerated in Maryland, he made a deliberate commitment to take his lessons seriously. After leaving maximum security in 2007, he studied for several years with John Gray-Bey (Mo’J) and Richard Harris-El (Mo’Shinmey) at E.C.I. in Westover, Maryland.

Enslaved Minds brings his reflections on those teachings to the page.
